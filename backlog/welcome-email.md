# Frist 7 days of Onboarding

- During onboarding and the first 7 days of the free trial, we need to be able to send different emails to the user to get them to progress steadily through account setup. 

Here is the high-level onboarding plan:

--- 

Refined Onboarding Plan

## Phase 1: The Setup (Day 1)
Child Profiles: Name, School, Year Group, and Key Clubs (e.g., "Rugby," "Drama"). Why: This trains the AI on what is 'Signal' vs 'Noise'.

Claim your Assistant: "Choose your dedicated address: [Name]@inbox.getfamilyassistant.com"

The "Quick Start" Forward: "Forward just your last 3 school emails (per child) right now. We’ll process them in 60 seconds." Try to include one with lots of information, like newsletters and schedule or Google classroom homework summary emails.

The Instant Win: Send a "Welcome Briefing" immediately. It should look beautiful and say: "I found a Trip Deadline on Friday and a PTA meeting on Tuesday. I've drafted these for your calendar."

## Phase 2: The Deep Dive (Day 2-5)
The Automation Nudge: A video tutorial or 3-step guide on setting up Gmail/Outlook filters to auto-forward any mail from @school.sch.uk.

The "Magic Feature" Intro: Day 4 email: "Did you know you can snap a photo of a paper flyer and send it to your assistant?"

The Partner Invite: Suggest adding a spouse or nanny to the briefing list (starts building the "Family" value).

## Phase 3: The Conversion (Day 6-7)
Day 6: The Preview. "Your trial ends tomorrow. Here is a summary of the 14 deadlines I tracked for you this week."

Day 7: The "Paywall" with Grace. "Trial ended. We’ve saved your Child Profiles and Assistant settings. Subscribe to keep your Monday Morning Briefing coming."
